# Product

## Register

product

## Users

TutorConnect serves students, parents, tutors, and admins in the Vietnamese private tutoring market, initially focused on Hanoi and Ho Chi Minh City. Students and parents use the product to find verified tutors, compare fit, understand price and availability, book sessions, pay, message, and review completed lessons. Tutors use it to build a credible profile, manage availability, accept bookings, communicate with learners, receive payment, and request withdrawals. Admins use it to verify tutor profiles, monitor bookings and payments, resolve disputes, and keep the marketplace safe and operational.

## Product Purpose

TutorConnect is an end-to-end tutor booking marketplace. Its purpose is to make tutor discovery, booking, payment, and operational review clear enough that parents and students can trust the transaction, tutors can manage their work professionally, and admins can oversee the marketplace without ambiguity. Success means verified tutor supply, fast and understandable booking decisions, transparent payment and escrow states, fewer avoidable disputes, and admin workflows that surface the right operational risk at the right time.

## Brand Personality

The product should feel trustworthy, clear, local, calm, and expert. The tone is direct, helpful, and reassuring, with enough professionalism for tutors and admins and enough plain-language clarity for students and parents. The experience should feel grounded in the Vietnamese market rather than like a generic global SaaS template.

## Anti-references

Avoid gimmicky edtech visuals, childish illustrations, generic SaaS gradients, vague marketing language, decorative dashboards, excessive animation, and overly playful interaction patterns. Do not let the marketplace feel like a full LMS, video learning platform, or AI-first product. Avoid UI that hides booking terms, pricing, tutor verification, cancellation policy, payment status, or admin decision history behind vague labels.

## Design Principles

1. Make trust visible at decision points: tutor verification, credentials, ratings, availability, pricing, payment status, and admin decisions should be explicit and easy to scan.
2. Keep marketplace workflows legible: discovery, booking, payment, cancellation, messaging, review, and withdrawal flows should always show current status and next action.
3. Write for parents and students first, then support expert users with density: plain Vietnamese labels and validation should guide critical actions without slowing admins and tutors.
4. Prefer operational clarity over decoration: dashboards, tables, filters, and status controls should prioritize accuracy, hierarchy, and auditability.
5. Protect MVP scope: improve the core booking marketplace loop before adding LMS, video, AI recommendation, subscription, or advanced analytics patterns.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use WCAG AA as the baseline. Prioritize readable Vietnamese typography, strong contrast, keyboard-accessible controls, visible focus states, clear form validation, reduced-motion alternatives, responsive layouts, and status communication that does not rely on color alone. Critical booking, payment, verification, and dispute states should be understandable for users with low technical confidence and for admins working through dense operational queues.
